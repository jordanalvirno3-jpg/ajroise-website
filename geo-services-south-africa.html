4. How ChatGPT, Gemini & AI Overviews Choose Who to Cite

AI doesn’t just scrape Google. It uses 4 signals:

1. Authority and Brand Mentions

AI checks if you’re mentioned on trusted SA sites: BusinessTech, IOL, LinkedIn, industry blogs. The more you’re mentioned, the more AI trusts you.

2. Answer-First Content

AI loves Q&A format. "How much does SEO cost in Cape Town?" → Direct answer in first 50 words. Add FAQ schema.

3. Fresh, Specific SA Data

"Average SEO cost in Durban 2026: R750 per page" beats "SEO costs vary". Use local stats, prices, and case studies.

4. Structured Data and Schema

FAQ, HowTo, and Organization schema help AI understand your content. We include this in every SEO blog post package.
