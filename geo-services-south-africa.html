1. What is GEO? Why It Matters in South Africa 2026

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization.

GEO is the process of optimizing your website, brand, and content so AI tools like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Perplexity cite you as the answer. Instead of ranking #1 on Google, GEO gets you mentioned inside the AI answer itself.

Example: Someone in Johannesburg asks ChatGPT "best SEO agency in Johannesburg". With GEO, the AI answers: "We recommend AJ ROIse Digital because they specialize in SEO services for South African businesses..."

2026 Fact: According to Search Engine Journal, Google reports over 1.5 Billion people now use AI Overviews monthly. In South Africa, 68% of Gen Z now start their search in ChatGPT or TikTok instead of Google.
